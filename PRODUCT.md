# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (index.html + own CSS/JS, GSAP via CDN, no build). Deploy: Vercel + private GitHub repo DevX4N/agrovant, `noindex` (conceptual project).

## Users

Primary visitor of this site: a potential client of a web development agency (AJ Solutions Tech) evaluating its portfolio. In-fiction audience: Brazilian rural producers, agricultural groups, agronomic consultancies, cooperatives and farm managers deciding whether to request a demo of a farm-data platform.

## Product Purpose

AGROVANT (fictional) is an AgTech company: a digital platform for monitoring rural properties — crop health (NDVI), climate, machinery, productivity indicators, alerts, an AI assistant over farm data, and integrations. The site is an institutional/marketing site whose job is to make the platform credible and drive "Solicitar demonstração". As a portfolio case, success means it reads as a real, consolidated, high-end AgTech brand.

## Positioning

Field + software + data: a technology platform that understands agribusiness deeply — not an agricultural company website. Name = AGRO + ADVANCE. Tagline: "Inteligência que nasce no campo."

## Operating Context

Brazilian agribusiness (soja/milho, safra notation like 2026/27, units sc/ha, ha, mm). Reference property: Fazenda Santa Helena (Mato Grosso). Other mock properties: Boa Esperança, São Lucas.

## Capabilities and Constraints

All product data, metrics, case numbers and the testimonial are fictional and must be labeled as demonstrative where they appear (brief supplies disclaimers). Lead form is simulated (no backend) and shows a success message. Copy is Portuguese (pt-BR). Footer credit: "Desenvolvido por AJ Solutions Tech."

## Brand Commitments

From the brief (binding): dark ground #080B09 / #101511 / cards #151B16; lime #B6F23A as accent only; secondary green #6F9C2C; text #F4F6F1 / #8E978F; borders rgba(255,255,255,.08); small touches of earth-beige, grey, white, olive. Modern sans (Manrope/Inter/Geist/Satoshi/DM Sans suggested), big compact headlines, tracked uppercase labels. Abstract tech logo mark (planting lines, coordinates, plots, satellites, letter A) — never a leaf. Avoid: excess green, decorative leaves, generic farm icons, rustic look, corporate templates, heavy gradients, childish look, smiling-farmer clichés.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. Photography: real aerial/drone farmland photos from Unsplash (free license), stored locally. No real customers, partners, certifications or press — do not invent any beyond the brief's labeled fictional content.

## Product Principles

1. The product proves itself: show the interface working, not claims.
2. Precision over decoration — every visual element should read as data, map or instrument.
3. Fiction is labeled: demonstrative numbers carry their disclaimer.
4. Field-ready clarity: legible, fast, works on a phone in the field.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, semantic HTML, keyboard-operable interactive mocks, prefers-reduced-motion respected.
